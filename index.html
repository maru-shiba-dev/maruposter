MaruPoster

MaruPoster is a personal tool that posts its owner's own snowboard and car-life videos to the owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

It is operated by an individual for personal, non-commercial use. It is used only with the owner's own TikTok account, after the owner authorizes the app on TikTok's official authorization screen.

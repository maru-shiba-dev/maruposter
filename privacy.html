Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

MaruPoster is a personal tool operated by an individual. This policy explains what information the tool handles.

Information the tool accesses

After the account owner authorizes the tool through TikTok's official Login Kit flow, the tool receives:

  • Basic account information (user ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope.
  • An access token used to upload and post the owner's own videos via the video.upload and video.publish scopes.

How the information is used

The information is used solely to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account at the owner's direction. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Sharing

No information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely by the operator and are deleted when the owner revokes authorization or when the tool is discontinued.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings → Security → Apps), which invalidates the stored tokens.
